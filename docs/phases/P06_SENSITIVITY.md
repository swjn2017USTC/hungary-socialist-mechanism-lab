## Inert axes dropped from the sensitivity ranking

The two trade-demand-vector axes were exercised at 0.8×/1.0×/1.2×. Every arm produced the same replay digest as the unperturbed baseline (`92f9ea0a…`) and identical measured terminal values; these axes are therefore inert on this run path and are excluded from the ranking. The preflight-rejected wage-rate 4.8 point is not treated as a result. The other completed perturbations changed replay digests, even when their terminal metrics did not move; that indicates an internal deterministic path change, not evidence of historical reproduction improvement.
